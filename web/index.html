How it works
  • Highlight any text in a reply and ask about it. The answer opens in a new pane next to the one it came from.
  • Numbered markers in the text show where each branch starts. Click one to open it.
  • In a branch, Back returns you to the exact spot it came from, and Takeaway adds a short summary to the parent.
  • Search finds everything: questions, answers, highlights, takeaways and documents. Typos are fine.
  • Zen is just the panes, for reading. The tab at the top brings the rest back.
  • Collapse any pane to a thin strip and click the strip to expand it again (drag a strip to move it). Collapse others (in a pane’s header) collapses every other pane; again, or Esc, brings them back.
  • Canvas: drag the background to pan and scroll to zoom; drag a card by its title to move it, or to an edge to dock it.
